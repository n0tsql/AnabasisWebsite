WAR WITH HELL

HELL DEVOURS THE INDOLENT.

Movement and speed are crucial in your divine struggle against the Blight. Run, jump, think, swing! A blur of motion will be all your enemy saw. Be restless, lest you be consumed by Hell as one of them. With your trusty Chainglaive at your side, you must be capable of manuevering chaotic arenas and killing anything, and mean anything, on sight. Your aggression is how you survive waves of the dead.

We are currently working on INFERNOMANIA, the premier action-based roguelike endless mode set in the city of Pandæmonium. Struggle to survive as you fight waves of endless Blight. Use your Chainglaive and new mobility tech to navigate the chaotic arenas, defeat top-tier wrasslin'-inspired bosses in Hell's hierarchy as you fight to live another wave.

Join our official Discord to stay updated on InfernoMania progress, events, and release!

IT'S YOU AGAINST THEM.

Divine scion of Ares and last champion of the Gods, you are their final hero. As you stand betwixt ages of man, you are alone 'gainst the tides of Hell. Prepare yourself accordingly, for you are a one man army. Equipment, loot, and armour you find across Hellas is your only defense — and offense — against the damned. No one is here to save you, for you must save yourself.

PUSH THEM BACK.

No war is without its frontlines. With every battle, the lines shift and stakes grow higher. This war is no longer about extermination, but about holding the line. Participate in large-scale PvE battles to defend Hellas, repel the Blight back, and take them directly to their homeworld: Hell.

SANCTIFY THE EARTH.

Purify the realm against the encroaching Blight, sanctify the holy grounds of Hellas and keep the Blight at bay. With Purification potions, you can cleanse the land and uproot the corruption. Purification is also a versatile tool in combat — a potion is enough to kill many Blighted.

THE GODS CAN'T HELP.

Heaven stands besieged by the Titan King's Tyrants, as demonic Legions ascend and breathe the ether, you are left to fight for not just your survival, but the salvation of all mankind. You lead your mortal Katabasis alone, the final stand against Kronos' dominion over damnation.

Hell

The realm of torture and punishment for sinners, layered one through ten in rank of severity.

Aprothymus

Last hero of the Gods and champion of mankind. Raiser of an army two million strong to conquer Hell.

Kronos

Titan King and devourer of his own children, he leads the Hell insurrection against the Gods with his Tyrants.

Hellas

Home of the Hellenes, the cradle of civilisation under threat by the Blight. They are your people, and you are destined to protect them.

Polum

Hellenic citystate and founder of the Hellenic League, the defensive alliance against the spreading Blight.

The Chainglaive

Your trusty spear-on-a-chain weapon. Use it to traverse the treacherous terrain of corrupted Hellas, or to grapple your enemies.
